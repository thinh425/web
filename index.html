My FPT University

FPT University (FPTU) is one of the premier private universities in Vietnam, established by the FPT Corporation. Renowned for its excellence in Information Technology, business, innovation, and global integration, FPTU provides students with a dynamic learning environment closely tied to industry demands and international standards.
